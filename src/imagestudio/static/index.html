What do these settings mean?
Preset
best is full resolution and full steps on the strongest model that fits. balanced and fast lower the resolution (FLUX) or the steps (Qwen-Image) to finish sooner, and each states its trade-off.
Resolution
Pixels cost time and memory. 1024×1024 is four times the work of 512×512. For sharp large prints, render at 1024 and upscale (coming in the next release).
Seed
Fixes the randomness. Keep the seed to reproduce or to tweak a prompt while keeping the composition; change it to explore.
Steps
Denoising passes. Distilled models like FLUX schnell are tuned for exactly 4; others improve up to a point (Qwen-Image around 50) and then only get slower.
Guidance (CFG)
How hard the model is pushed to obey the prompt. Low values are free and natural; high values obey more but look harsh. 1 means off and halves the work.
Sampler
euler: the standard, predictable, used by the presets. euler_a: adds noise each step; livelier, less reproducible across step counts. heun: two evaluations per step, slightly more accurate, twice the time. dpm2: similar idea to heun. dpm++2s_a / dpm++2m / dpm++2mv2: higher-order methods that converge in fewer steps on classic diffusion models; little benefit on 4-step FLUX. ipndm / ipndm_v: multistep predictors, smooth results at low step counts. lcm and tcd: for models distilled for those schedules. ddim_trailing: deterministic classic scheduler.
Quantisation
Bits per weight in the model file. q8 is visually identical to the original; q5 loses a little fine texture; q4 softens small text and detail. It changes memory, not speed.
Load meter
What the job needs against this machine's memory budget (RAM minus 2 GiB, plus VRAM of a discrete GPU). "will swap" runs, several times slower. "over budget" is refused unless you unlock it in config.json.
