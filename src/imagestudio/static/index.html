Glossary

Preset
best is full resolution and full steps on the strongest model that fits. balanced and fast lower the resolution (FLUX) or the steps (Qwen-Image) to finish sooner, and say so.
Resolution
Pixels cost time and memory. 1024² is four times the work of 512². For sharp large images render at 1024 and upscale (next release).
Seed
Fixes the randomness. Keep it to reproduce or to tweak a prompt while keeping the composition; change it to explore.
Steps
Denoising passes. Distilled models like FLUX schnell are tuned for exactly 4; others improve up to a point (Qwen-Image around 50), then only get slower.
Guidance (CFG)
How hard the model obeys the prompt. Low is natural; high obeys more but looks harsh. 1 means off and halves the work.
Samplers
euler standard, predictable. euler_a adds noise each step: livelier, less reproducible. heun, dpm2 two evaluations per step: a little more accurate, twice the time. dpm++2s_a, dpm++2m, dpm++2mv2 higher-order: converge in fewer steps on classic models, little gain on 4-step FLUX. ipndm, ipndm_v multistep predictors, smooth at low step counts. lcm, tcd for models distilled for those schedules. ddim_trailing deterministic classic scheduler.
Quantisation
Bits per weight in the file. q8 is visually identical to the original; q5 loses a little fine texture; q4 softens small text and detail. Changes memory, not speed.
Load meter
What the job needs against this machine's memory budget (RAM minus 2 GiB, plus a discrete GPU's VRAM). "will swap" runs, several times slower. "over budget" is refused unless you allow it in Settings.
